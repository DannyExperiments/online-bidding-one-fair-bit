# Status

[Version 1.0.0](https://github.com/DannyExperiments/online-bidding-one-fair-bit/releases/tag/v1.0.0) · 27 November 2026 · [Version DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24571176)

One fair bit gives an optimal worst-target expected competitive ratio of 27/8 in online bidding. More generally, with k equally likely seeds the optimum is (1+1/k)^(k+1).

The internal mathematical review covered the cyclic contract transfer, ties, stopping conventions and the direct logarithmic proof. The result is an attributed application of the classical contract lower bound. External peer review and formal verification have not been conducted. See the [mathematical review](audits/public_safe_reports/MATHEMATICS.md) and [source comparison](audits/public_safe_reports/LITERATURE.md).
