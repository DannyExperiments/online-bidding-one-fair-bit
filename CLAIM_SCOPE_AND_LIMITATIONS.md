# Claim scope

The target is at least one and is fixed independently of the seed. The bidder pays every bid through the first one at least as large as the target. Equal seed probabilities are part of the model. One fair bit gives two seeds, and b independent fair bits give 2^b seeds, with repeated schedules permitted.

The result gives an exact ratio for that model. It leaves arbitrary probabilities on a fixed number of schedules, adversaries that see the seed, advice, and other cost conventions outside the claim. The source review records the corresponding earlier scheduling theorem. No priority claim about the transfer is made.
