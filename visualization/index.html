Where the ratio comes from

Set a = 1 + 1/k. Seed i bids powers of a with exponents i, i+k, i+2k, and so on. Every bid below target is paid by its seed, and each seed pays its first bid at or above target.

Just above aʲ the k stopping bids have consecutive exponents j+1 through j+k. At aʲ itself the first stopping exponent is j, because a bid equal to the target succeeds. The right-limit ratio is aᵏ⁺¹ − a⁻ʲ and converges to aᵏ⁺¹.
